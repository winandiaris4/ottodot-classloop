Full Stack Software Engineer [Remote Role]

We are looking for a full-stack software engineer who can be hands-on and own engineering judgment. You will help us turn a fast-moving founder-built platform into a reliable product that students, parents, teachers, and ops can trust.

Build and improve student, parent, teacher, and admin workflows.
Stabilize login, account access, dashboards, class data, and homework flows.
